Done-For-You Outbound for Recruitment Agencies

By Abir, Founder of SDR GROW · July 2026 · 7 min read
← Back to Blog

Done-for-you outbound means someone else builds and runs your client acquisition machine. The sending domains, the lead lists, the sequences, the follow-ups. You take the meetings. That is the whole pitch, and for a lot of recruitment agencies it is the right call. For some it is not. This page covers what done-for-you actually includes, what outbound actually costs, when it beats doing it yourself, and when it does not.

What Does Done-For-You Outbound Actually Include?

The term gets thrown around loosely, so pin down what you are buying before you sign anything. A real done-for-you setup covers six jobs:

Sending infrastructure. Separate domains bought and configured for outreach, so your main agency domain never gets burned. SPF, DKIM and DMARC records set up correctly. Inboxes warmed for two to three weeks before a single cold email goes out. Skip this and everything else lands in spam.

Lead sourcing. Someone builds your target list. Not a CSV export from a database, but decision makers at companies that actually hire in your niche, with verified emails. For recruitment agencies the good providers pull every layer: the founder, the hiring manager, the HR lead, not just one contact per company.

Sequence writing. The messages themselves. This is where most providers are weakest, because generic B2B copy does not work for recruitment. Your prospects get forty cold emails a week from agencies. The copy has to reference their open roles, their niche, their pain, not your years in the market.

Multichannel execution. Email alone caps you. The providers worth paying combine email with LinkedIn touches, profile views, connection requests, engagement, so the prospect has seen your name three times before the direct ask lands.

Reply handling and booking. Some providers stop at the reply and hand it to you. Others qualify and book the meeting. Know which one you are buying, because the difference is hours of your week.

Reporting. Open rates, reply rates, meetings booked, and what changed month to month. If a provider will not show you the numbers, walk.

What Does Done-For-You Outbound Cost?

Three tiers exist in this market:

Offshore agencies, $500 to $1,500 a month. Usually email only, template heavy, high volume. Some get results in forgiving niches. Recruitment is not a forgiving niche, because your buyers are marketed to constantly and smell a template instantly.

Specialist systems, $1,200 to $3,500 a month. Built for one vertical, infrastructure plus execution, usually with a setup fee between $1,500 and $3,000. This is where SDR GROW sits: $1,997 setup, $1,200 a month, with an optional VA at $2,000 a month if you want zero hands on it. Full disclosure, that is our product, and this page exists partly because people search this exact phrase.

Full-service SDR agencies, $4,000 to $10,000 a month. A dedicated rep or pod working your account, often with calling included. Strong if you have the budget and a proven offer. Overkill for most agencies under $2M revenue, and you are paying salaries with margin on top.

Compare all three against the do-it-yourself number: an in-house SDR runs $4,000 to $6,000 a month before tools and takes three to six months to produce anything. Assembling the tool stack yourself costs roughly $1,500 a month in software plus over 100 hours a month of labour. Once you count the hours honestly, done-for-you at $1,200 to $3,500 is usually the cheapest working option, not the expensive one.

When Done-For-You Beats Doing It Yourself

Done-for-you wins when at least two of these are true:

Your billable time is worth more than the outbound work. A recruiter who bills placements should not be spending 25 hours a month building lists.

You have no one in-house who enjoys tooling. Outbound stacks reward an operator who likes configuring things. Most agencies do not have that person, and forcing a recruiter into the role fails within a month.

You have stopped and started outbound before. If your agency has burned two or three attempts at cold email, the problem is usually consistency, not the channel. A system that runs whether or not you are busy fixes the actual failure mode.

You need it working in weeks, not quarters. Setup on a specialist system takes days. An SDR hire takes a quarter to ramp, if the hire works out at all.

When It Does Not

Skip done-for-you if your offer itself is unproven. No outbound system fixes a service nobody wants. If you have never closed a client from any channel, fix positioning first.

Skip it if you cannot handle the meetings. Outbound that books eight meetings a month is wasted on a founder with no time to take them.

And skip the cheap tier entirely. Recruitment prospects are the most cold-emailed buyers in B2B. Template blasts do not just fail, they burn your name in a market where everyone knows everyone.

Questions to Ask Any Provider Before You Pay

Do you use separate sending domains, and who owns them when we part ways? The right answer is separate domains that you keep.

How many contacts per target company do you reach? One contact per company is a coin flip. Every relevant layer is a system.

How many touches over how long? Most replies in recruitment outbound arrive between touch 5 and touch 12. A provider running four-touch sequences is leaving most of your meetings unclaimed.

Who writes the copy and how is it tailored to recruitment? Ask to see a sequence they have run for another agency.

What happens to my data if I leave? Lists, domains and copy should be yours.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does done-for-you outbound cost for a recruitment agency?

Between $500 and $10,000 a month depending on tier. The workable middle for most agencies is $1,200 to $3,500 a month plus a setup fee, which still lands well under the $4,000 to $6,000 monthly cost of one in-house SDR.

How long until a done-for-you system books meetings?

Domains need two to three weeks of warming before sending starts, so expect first replies in weeks three to four and a steady meeting flow from week six onward. Any provider promising meetings in week one is skipping the warming and will burn the domains.

Is done-for-you outbound better than hiring an SDR?

For agencies under roughly $2M revenue, usually yes. An SDR costs more, takes months to ramp, and takes the process with them when they leave. The sensible order is system first, then hire someone to run and extend it once it is proven.

What reply rate should a done-for-you provider deliver?

Generic outbound sits at 1 to 2 percent. A tightly targeted multichannel sequence should aim for 8 to 15 percent. Treat any provider guaranteeing a specific number as a red flag, because results depend on niche, list quality and your offer.
