How to Find Companies That Just Posted New Roles (2026)

By Abir, Founder of SDR GROW · July 2026 · 4 min read
← Back to Blog

Direct answer: Fresh job postings are found through four sources, in order of freshness: company career pages checked directly, LinkedIn job feeds filtered by date and niche, aggregator boards with posting date filters, and hiring announcement posts by founders and managers, which often precede the formal listing by days. The operational question is not where the postings live but how fast your system moves from posting to first touch, because a role posted this week is a live wound and the same role at week six is a resigned one.

Key takeaways

  • Posting age is the qualifier: the first two weeks are the outreach window with the most energy in it.
  • The manager's "we're hiring" post beats the job board listing by days. Watch people, not just boards.
  • Volume patterns matter more than single postings: three roles in a month is a story.
  • Speed from detection to touch is the metric that separates systems from habits.

Where exactly do the freshest signals live?

Career pages first: companies post there before syndication, and a weekly crawl of your mapped targets' pages catches roles days before boards do. LinkedIn second, two layers deep: the jobs feed filtered by date, function and geography, plus the human layer, hiring managers posting "excited to grow the team," which frequently lands before any listing exists. Aggregators third, with date filters set tight. And the indirect layer: funding news and expansion announcements, which are postings in their larval stage. Manually, this is hours weekly and the first discipline abandoned in a busy month, which is why the SDR GROW lead engine attaches live posting data to every mapped company as standard, and the sequence's first lines cite the roles without anyone patrolling boards at night.

What do posting patterns tell you beyond the posting?

Read the shape, not just the fact. One senior role posted quietly: a replacement, possibly sensitive, approach with tact. Three plus roles in a function within a month: growth strain, budget approved, the classic agency moment. A role reposted or open past five weeks: a struggling search, the sharpest pain and the best first line in the niche. Roles across multiple functions at once: a funded scale up staffing to a plan, worth mapping every layer immediately. The pattern is the qualification; the posting is just its evidence.

Run the numbers on a working week.

A boutique maps 350 target companies. The weekly signal pass surfaces: 41 companies with active postings, of which 12 show fresh postings inside two weeks, 5 show volume patterns of three plus roles, and 3 show aged postings past five weeks. The week's outreach allocation follows the math: the 12 fresh enter sequences with role citing first lines, the 5 volume cases get multi layer mapping and priority, the 3 aged cases get the tactful pain line, and the untouched 309 stay warm in nurture. Twenty prospects, each entered at the moment their situation made the message welcome. That is a targeting week, and no part of it required luck.

Checklist: posting signal operations

  • Mapped targets' career pages crawled at least weekly.
  • Date filtered feeds tuned to your niche and geography.
  • Hiring manager posts monitored, not just listings.
  • Posting age and volume patterns logged per company.
  • Detection to first touch time under one week, measured.
  • Sequences updated when a prospect's postings change mid flight.

Mistakes to avoid

  • Treating all postings as equal. Age and pattern carry the meaning.
  • Citing a posting you did not verify is still live. Filled roles in first lines end conversations.
  • Watching boards only, and meeting every rival at the same listings.
  • Detecting signals on Monday and sending in three weeks. The window is the value.

FAQ

Are postings a saturated signal, since every agency can see them??

The postings are public; the speed, the pattern reading and the multi layer follow through are not. Most rivals send one email to one HR inbox a month late.

Should we contact companies with no postings at all??

Yes, through nurture and other signals. Posting outreach is the sharp edge, not the whole knife.

How do we verify a posting is still open??

Check the source page the day the touch sends. Systems do this automatically; humans should calendar it.
