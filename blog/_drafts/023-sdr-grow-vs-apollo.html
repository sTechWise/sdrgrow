SDR GROW vs Apollo for Recruitment Agencies (2026)

By Abir Hossain · July 2026 · 3 min read
← Back to Blog

Direct answer: Apollo is a huge contact database with basic sequencing bolted on. SDR GROW is a complete outbound system built for recruitment agencies. Choose Apollo when you want raw data and will build the strategy yourself. Choose SDR GROW when you want the whole job done: mapped decision makers, both channels, and the intelligence layer around the sending.

Key takeaways

  • Apollo's strength is database size. Its weakness is everything after the export.
  • Generic sequences underperform in recruitment, one of the most cold emailed niches.
  • Apollo covers email and calls. LinkedIn stays manual.
  • Price per seat looks cheap until you add the missing pieces.

What does Apollo do well?

Scale. Hundreds of millions of contacts, strong filters, sequences and a dialer in one login, from $49 per user per month. For a team that knows outbound and needs a data source, it earns its place. Recruitment agencies hit two problems. Data accuracy varies by region and role, and bounces from stale data burn sending domains fast. And the sequencing is generic sales style, built for software vendors, not for an agency selling staffing services.

What does SDR GROW do differently?

It starts where Apollo stops. The lead engine maps every decision layer at a target company and verifies each contact before anything sends. The email pipeline handles domains, warmup and rotation, the part Apollo leaves to you. The 16 touch flow coordinates email and LinkedIn over 60 days. Then the intelligence modules do work Apollo has no equivalent for: Competitor Mentions flags prospects unhappy with rival recruiters, Industry Insight feeds current market signals into first lines, LinkedIn Spy and Blog Spy hand you proven content topics, and Brand Voice keeps every message sounding like your firm. SDR GROW costs more, $1,997 setup plus $1,200 a month, and covers roughly six jobs Apollo does not.

Checklist: which one fits you

  • You have an operator who lives in outbound tools: Apollo works.
  • Nobody at the firm owns outbound: system wins.
  • LinkedIn matters in your market: Apollo needs a second tool.
  • Your domain reputation is precious: verified multi layer mapping wins.
  • You want intent signals, not just contacts: Apollo has none.

Example

An agency exports 2,000 contacts from a database tool and runs a generic sequence. Bounces climb, replies do not. The same agency later runs 300 mapped companies, three verified contacts each, both channels, with first lines tied to open roles. Fewer contacts, more meetings. Data volume lost to system quality.

Mistakes to avoid

  • Judging tools by database size instead of meeting count.
  • Sending to exported lists without verification.
  • Running Apollo sequences written for SaaS sales at hiring managers.
  • Forgetting LinkedIn because your data tool does not do it.

FAQ

Can I use Apollo and SDR GROW together??

You will not need to. Lead sourcing is built into the system. Some agencies keep Apollo for ad hoc research.

Is Apollo data good enough for recruitment outbound??

Sometimes. Verify before sending, always. Bounces cost more than any subscription.

Which is cheaper in total??

Apollo alone, yes. Apollo plus a LinkedIn tool, verification, copy hours and glue work narrows the gap fast.
