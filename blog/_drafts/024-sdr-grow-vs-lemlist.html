SDR GROW vs lemlist for Recruitment Agencies (2026)

By Abir Hossain · July 2026 · 4 min read
← Back to Blog

Direct answer: lemlist is a strong multichannel tool you operate. SDR GROW is a full outbound system operated for you and by you together. Both cover email and LinkedIn in one sequence, which puts them ahead of email only tools. The difference is everything around the sequence: who finds the leads, who writes the strategy, who watches the market. With lemlist that person is you. With SDR GROW it is the system.

Key takeaways

  • Both run email and LinkedIn in one sequence. That is where the similarity ends.
  • lemlist charges per user. SDR GROW charges per firm.
  • Personalization features do not replace targeting strategy.
  • The intelligence layer is the widest gap between them.

What does lemlist do well?

Personalization and multichannel steps. Custom images, liquid syntax, LinkedIn touches beside email touches, a built in lead database, warmup included, from $69 per user per month. For a small team that enjoys building campaigns, it is one of the best self serve tools on the market. What it does not do: decide which recruitment agencies' clients to target, map the three decision layers at each company, tell you a prospect just complained about a rival firm, or keep your messaging tied to this month's market. Those jobs stay with you.

What does SDR GROW cover that lemlist leaves open?

Five of its eight modules have no lemlist equivalent. The lead engine builds multi layer contact maps per target company, verified. Competitor Mentions catches prospects showing frustration with rival recruiters, the warmest signal in the market. Industry Insight feeds live market shifts into your first lines. LinkedIn Spy and Blog Spy turn competitor content performance into your weekly content plan. Brand Voice keeps every message in one recognizable voice across channels and touches. The email pipeline and 16 touch flow then match lemlist's core, with recruitment specific sequencing instead of a blank canvas. SDR GROW runs $1,997 setup plus $1,200 a month against lemlist's per seat pricing.

Checklist: which one fits you

  • You like building campaigns yourself: lemlist.
  • You want meetings, not a hobby: system.
  • You need intent signals and market feeds: system, lemlist has none.
  • Budget under $100 a month: lemlist, and accept the labor.
  • More than 3 seats needed: compare total prices again. Per seat adds up.

Example

A three person agency runs lemlist. The campaigns look great. Reply rates stay thin because targeting is a guess: one contact per company, generic lists, no signals. The problem was never the tool. It was the six jobs around the tool that nobody owned. A system owns them by design.

Mistakes to avoid

  • Confusing pretty personalization with relevant targeting.
  • Sequencing one contact per company.
  • Buying per seat tools for a growing team without doing the math forward.
  • Leaving market watching to memory. Memory watches nothing.

FAQ

Is lemlist good for recruiters specifically??

It is good for anyone and specific to no one. Recruitment niches reward specific.

Does SDR GROW personalize as deeply as lemlist??

It personalizes on what moves replies in recruitment: open roles, market events, rival complaints. Signal based lines outperform decorative ones.

Can I switch from lemlist without losing my history??

Your reply history lives in your inboxes either way. Sequences rebuild in days during setup.
