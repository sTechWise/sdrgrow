← Back to Blog

Instantly is the easiest way to send your first cold email campaign, which is exactly why so many recruitment agency owners start there, and why many stall there too. Here's the honest comparison with SDR GROW.

Where Instantly wins

Simplicity and entry price. Clean interface, built-in warm-up, campaigns live in an afternoon, ~$37/month. As a low-risk way to learn how cold email works mechanically, it's the best on the market.

Where SDR GROW wins

Results in a competitive niche. Recruitment is one of the most cold-emailed industries on earth, and single-channel template outreach there typically earns the classic 1–2% reply rate. What moves replies to the 8–15% range is exactly what a beginner tool doesn't provide: intent-based targeting, every decision layer of a company touched rather than one inbox, and 16 coordinated touches across email and LinkedIn over 60 days, because most replies come between touch 5 and touch 12, after a two-email campaign has already given up. SDR GROW also removes the part beginners get wrong most: deliverability infrastructure, set up properly from day one so your main domain never burns.

The verdict

Choose Instantly to experiment cheaply and learn the mechanics. Choose SDR GROW when the goal is actual client meetings this quarter and you'd rather run a system than build one. Start with the 7-day free trial, full access, no card, and watch it work before paying.
