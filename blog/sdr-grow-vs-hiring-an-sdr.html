← Back to Blog

When a recruitment agency decides to take client acquisition seriously, the instinct is to hire: a business development rep, an SDR, someone to "do outbound." Sometimes that's right. Often it's an expensive way to discover you didn't have a system for them to run. Here's the real cost comparison and honest math.

The case for hiring

A great SDR does things no system can: real phone conversations, relationship nuance, live objection handling, intelligence from the market. Agencies past roughly $2M revenue with steady deal flow often should have a human closer-of-meetings, the leverage compounds. And a person can sell in channels software can't touch, like industry events.

The case for the system

Sequence and probability. The standard failure mode is hiring an SDR before a working outbound system exists, the new hire spends months building lists, guessing at messaging, and fighting spam filters, at $5,000/month, and if they leave, everything leaves with them. A system inverts this: infrastructure, targeting, and sequencing exist first and keep running. SDR GROW's specific version, multi-layer contacts per target company, 16 touches across email and LinkedIn over 60 days, deliverability handled, produces the output of an SDR desk operated by one existing team member. And when you later do hire, the hire walks into a machine that already books meetings, which is also how you attract better reps.

The verdict

Hire first if you're a larger agency with existing outbound infrastructure and need a human to work the phones and close. System first for everyone else: prove the pipeline with SDR GROW at a quarter of an SDR's cost, then hire into a working machine.
