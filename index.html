What Are the Common Outbound Mistakes Recruitment Agencies Make?

These patterns hold agencies back from building predictable pipeline.

01

Sending Generic Templates to Everyone

Mass emails with no personalization get ignored. Each prospect needs context-specific messaging that shows you understand their business.

02

Stopping After One or Two Touches

Most replies happen between touch 5 and touch 12. Agencies that stop after two emails leave meetings on the table.

03

Using Only One Channel

Email alone caps your reach. Adding LinkedIn creates multiple visibility points and builds familiarity before the first conversation.

04

No Competitive Intelligence

Agencies that ignore competitor activity miss high-intent prospects. Monitoring competitor mentions reveals deals you can intercept.

05

Hiring SDRs Before Building a System

A new hire without a proven system is a gamble. Build the infrastructure first, then hand it to a person who can operate it.

06

Ignoring Email Deliverability

Without proper domain warming, SPF, DKIM, and DMARC setup, your emails land in spam. Technical infrastructure matters more than copy.
